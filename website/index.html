Everything you need to journal freely

Built with privacy, beauty and flexibility in mind.

Rich text editor

Write with formatting, colors, headings, quotes and embed images, videos and audio recordings.

Organize your thoughts

Attach tags to entries, sort by latest first, oldest first or A-Z and never lose a memory.

Cloud backups

Back up to Google Drive, Dropbox or your own Nextcloud server. Your data stays in your hands.

End-to-end encryption

Protect sensitive notes with optional encryption. Only you hold the keys to your private journal.

PIN & fingerprint lock

Secure the whole app with a PIN or your fingerprint so your diary stays for your eyes only.

Auto-save & reminders

Entries save automatically as you write. Set daily reminders to keep your writing habit alive.

Custom themes & fonts

Choose from built-in themes or build your own color palette. Customize fonts for the app and per note.

Export anywhere

Export notes to plain text or PDF whenever you want a backup outside the app.

Read notes aloud

Let the app read your entries back to you with text-to-speech and a voice you choose.
